## The 100 📺
A post-apocalyptic sci-fi series about 100 juvenile delinquents sent to Earth after nuclear apocalypse.
>
## Synopsis
Ninety-seven years after a devastating nuclear apocalypse wiped out almost all life on Earth, humankind is living in space aboard the Ark, a massive space station. When life-support systems begin to fail, 100 juvenile prisoners are sent to the surface to test whether Earth is habitable. The teens arrive to discover a beautiful planet, but they quickly realize they are not alone. Survivors of the nuclear disaster have been living on the ground, and a battle for survival begins as the young heroes face hostile grounders, dangerous wildlife, and the moral complexities of rebuilding civilization.
